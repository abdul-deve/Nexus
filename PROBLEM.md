## Problem Definition

#### 1. What Problem are we going to solve?

Small and medium-sized organizations often manage different types of work, such as projects, leaves, attendance, expenses, and payroll. Many organizations still handle these tasks using paper, spreadsheets, or manual processes. As a result, these processes can be error-prone and time-consuming.

As organizations grow, it becomes increasingly difficult to manage all these tasks manually

#### 2. Who has this problem?

The primary users are organization-admin. They need to manage their organizational-staff and basic organizational tasks.


#### 3.  Why is it a problem?
When an organization grows, it becomes difficult to keep track of what is going on, who is working on what, what has been done, and what remains. Tracking regular attendance, expenses, projects, and payroll manually, As a resultwe can hit errors like missing-data, wrong-data , delay in task and doubt-ful results

#### 4.What should change?
Through Nexus, we can resolve all these issues in a reliable way. We will be able to track every single action instantly and control who can do what. All logs and audits will be handled properly, making the whole process less time-consuming.
